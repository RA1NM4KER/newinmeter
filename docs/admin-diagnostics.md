# Admin Diagnostics

`/admin/diagnostics` is NewinMeter's compact operational view. The page and its JSON endpoint
(`/api/admin/diagnostics`) both call `requireAdminSession`; non-admins receive a server-side denial,
not merely a hidden navigation item.

## What it shows

- overall, LiveMopay-canary, and auto-sync scheduler health;
- real non-demo current connections, ordered critical → warning → healthy;
- last successful sync, last attempt, last automatic attempt, next schedule, stuck claim, stale
  state, failure streak, and eight recent `capture_runs`;
- recent operational incidents/recoveries from `system_events`; and
- active push-subscription count plus the latest push-delivery state.

Diagnostics deliberately selects no refresh-token ciphertext/IV/auth tag, Firebase token,
LiveMopay password, upstream account/property/company/device ID, push endpoint/key, service-role
key, VAPID private key, or cron secret. Error text and event metadata are sanitized at the storage
and serialization boundaries.

## Health rules

### Scheduler

The pg_cron worker is expected every five minutes. Each successful invocation overwrites one
`system_health_state` heartbeat; it does not create a permanent event per tick.

- **Healthy:** heartbeat no more than 15 minutes old.
- **Warning:** over 15 minutes old.
- **Critical:** over 30 minutes old or no heartbeat has ever been recorded.

An automatic-sync claim older than 15 minutes is critical for that connection (the claim RPC's
reclaim TTL remains 10 minutes). The Diagnostics page detects scheduler delay immediately when
opened. A critical scheduler push is generated by the daily watchdog in the canary job; a stopped
pg_cron system cannot invoke its own watchdog, so push detection resolution is intentionally up to
one day. The next resumed scheduler invocation records recovery.

### LiveMopay contract canary

- **Healthy:** latest attempt passed first time and the last success is at most 36 hours old.
- **Warning:** first attempt failed but the one bounded retry passed, or last success is 36–48
  hours old.
- **Critical:** both attempts failed, no success exists, or the last success is over 48 hours old.

The retry waits five seconds. The check runs once daily and performs only NewinMeter's production
dependency surface:

1. Firebase password login: ID token, refresh token, and valid expiry required.
2. Firebase refresh exchange: refreshed ID token, refresh token, and valid expiry required.
3. `GET /mobile/`: configured canary account plus account/company/property identity required.
4. `GET /mobile/ledger/{startDate}?accountId=...`: bounded to seven recent days; array shape,
   required row fields, and at least one row understood by the current parser required.

No extra endpoints from `docs/livemopay-api.md` are probed. Tokens and ledger rows are discarded;
only safe counts and the failed step are retained.

### Connections

- **Healthy:** connected, recent success within eight hours, no current failed attempt, no overdue
  schedule, and no stuck claim/failure streak.
- **Warning:** one or two consecutive failures, first sync pending, last success over eight hours
  old, latest automatic attempt failed, or the next schedule is over 30 minutes overdue.
- **Critical:** reauthentication required, three or more consecutive failures, success over 24
  hours old, a claim over 15 minutes old, no first success after 24 hours, or schedule over two
  hours overdue.

One transient sync failure remains visible in capture history but does not open a critical incident
or send an admin push.

## Events and operational pushes

`system_events` is for state transitions that do not belong to a routine capture run. The partial
unique index on unresolved `incident_key` permits only one open incident for a logical problem.
Repeated evaluations of the same critical state therefore do not create duplicate events or
pushes. Recovery resolves the open row and records one concise recovery event.

Admin pushes are sent only for:

- canary failure after retry;
- scheduler heartbeat critical when the daily watchdog evaluates it;
- three or more consecutive failures for one connection;
- connection reauthentication required; and
- at least three failures affecting at least half of a scheduler batch.

Deep links use only internal NewinMeter event/connection UUIDs and open
`/admin/diagnostics?connection=...`. Alert-family and hard push-delivery failures are warning
events visible in Diagnostics but do not themselves generate recursive operational pushes.

## Production setup

1. Apply `supabase/migrations/20260825220423_admin_diagnostics.sql` after the existing migrations.
2. Configure `NEWINMETER_CANARY_EMAIL`, `NEWINMETER_CANARY_PASSWORD`, and
   `NEWINMETER_CANARY_ACCOUNT_ID` as server-only deployment variables.
3. Keep the existing `newinmeter_auto_sync_secret` Vault secret equal to `CRON_SECRET`.
4. Add the canary route URL to Vault:

   ```sql
   select vault.create_secret(
     'https://your-deployment.vercel.app/api/cron/livemopay-canary',
     'newinmeter_livemopay_canary_url'
   );
   ```

5. Verify the `newinmeter-livemopay-canary` job is active at `30 2 * * *` and inspect
   `cron.job_run_details` after its first run.

The canary account should be dedicated, minimally privileged where LiveMopay permits, and active
enough to produce at least one parseable ledger entry inside the seven-day window. No canary
credential belongs in Supabase Vault, source control, logs, events, API responses, or browser
configuration.
